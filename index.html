The PVC Direct promise

When demand for PVC trim was rising so fast there was a national shortage, we were builders ourselves, sourcing PVC from lumberyards all over to keep our jobs going. After a two-hour drive to buy what seemed like the last 1x12s in the state, mixing brands with different shades of white, we dreamed up a store that sold just PVC on the ride home. That's how PVC Direct was born.

Today we stock thousands of trim boards at two yards and deliver everything we sell: on time, where you want it, and kept perfectly clean. Our whole company is built around the problems we saw as builders, and we take care of our customers like family.

No rot, no bugs

Water and moisture resistant, so there's no decay, rot or insect damage to worry about.

Works like wood

Cut, rout and fasten it with the same methods as wood. No paint needed, and it holds paint well if you want it.

Lasts longer

Far less upkeep than wood over its life, and it's made without cutting down forests.
